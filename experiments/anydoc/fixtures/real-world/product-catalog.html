Aster 8

The Aster 8 is an 8 kW air-to-water heat pump intended for well-insulated homes with up to three heating zones. Its standard paired hot-water cylinder is 180 litres.

  • Nominal output: 8 kW at A7/W35
  • Seasonal coefficient of performance (SCOP): 4.6 in the reference climate
  • Outdoor sound power: 52 dB(A)
  • Refrigerant: R-32, factory charge 1.15 kg
  • Manufacturer: Norrby Climate Systems

Aster 12

The Aster 12 is a 12 kW air-to-water heat pump intended for larger homes and small commercial premises with up to five heating zones. Its standard paired hot-water cylinder is 250 litres.

  • Nominal output: 12 kW at A7/W35
  • Seasonal coefficient of performance (SCOP): 4.3 in the reference climate
  • Outdoor sound power: 55 dB(A)
  • Refrigerant: R-32, factory charge 1.65 kg
  • Manufacturer: Norrby Climate Systems

Shared controls and warranty

Both models use the Atlas Link controller and support weather compensation, scheduled hot-water heating, and five configurable operating periods per day.

Both models carry a standard five-year product warranty from commissioning. CarePlus may extend that term, but eligibility is governed exclusively by the current Customer Support and Warranty Policy.

Specifications are nominal laboratory values. A site survey and heat-loss calculation are required before model selection.
