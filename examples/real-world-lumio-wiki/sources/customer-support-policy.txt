ATLAS HEATWORKS
CUSTOMER SUPPORT AND WARRANTY POLICY
Policy owner: Maya Chen, Operations Director
Revision: 3.2
Effective: 1 February 2026

1. SUPPORT PRIORITIES

Priority 1 (P1): Complete loss of heating or hot water while the outdoor temperature is below 5°C, a refrigerant alarm, or an electrical safety concern. Customer Support must acknowledge a P1 case within 30 minutes. The responsible service hub must provide remote or on-site technical response within 4 hours.

Priority 2 (P2): Reduced heating performance, recurring pressure loss, unusual compressor noise, or loss of one heating zone while basic heating remains available. Customer Support must respond within 1 business day.

Priority 3 (P3): Documentation requests, billing questions, app access, cosmetic damage, or non-urgent scheduling. Customer Support must respond within 3 business days.

2. STANDARD PRODUCT WARRANTY

Every Aster 8 and Aster 12 installation includes a five-year product warranty beginning on the commissioning date. Workmanship is covered for two years. Consumables, accidental damage, unauthorized modification, and missed maintenance are excluded as described in the customer agreement.

3. CAREPLUS EXTENSION

CarePlus extends the product warranty from five years to seven years only when all of the following are true:

- the system is registered within 30 calendar days after commissioning;
- commissioning was completed by an Atlas-certified installer;
- one documented preventive-maintenance visit is completed in every 12-month service period; and
- only approved replacement parts and refrigerant procedures are used.

A late annual service does not immediately cancel CarePlus. The customer has a 45-day cure period after Atlas sends the overdue notice. If service remains incomplete after that period, coverage returns to the standard five-year term. Customer Support may not promise an exception; exceptions require written approval from the Operations Director.

4. ROUTING

Cases are assigned by installation postcode to East Yard or West Yard. When the assigned hub cannot meet a P1 target, the duty coordinator may transfer the case to the other hub. Transfers must remain visible in the case record.

5. RECORDS

The commissioning record establishes the warranty start date. Annual service records establish continuing CarePlus eligibility. Support conversations do not replace either record.
