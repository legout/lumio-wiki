# Atlas Heatworks: company and service network

**Document owner:** Operations Office  
**Effective date:** 2026-01-15  
**Classification:** Internal reference

Atlas Heatworks is a fictional employee-owned heat-pump installer founded in 2018. The company serves homes and small commercial buildings across Alder County from its headquarters in Cedar Falls.

## Operating locations

- **Cedar Falls headquarters** — scheduling, customer support, finance, and the training workshop.
- **East Yard service hub** — covers Cedar Falls, Briar Glen, and the eastern rural district.
- **West Yard service hub** — covers Lakehurst, Pine Crossing, and the western rural district.

## Products and programs

Atlas Heatworks installs the **Aster 8** and **Aster 12** air-to-water heat-pump systems. Both models are manufactured for Atlas by **Norrby Climate Systems** and use R-32 refrigerant.

The **CarePlus** program extends the standard product warranty when the customer registers the installation on time and maintains an annual service record. Detailed eligibility rules are controlled by the Customer Support Policy, not by this overview.

The **BrightHome Access Fund** provides income-qualified households with installation grants. It is funded from one percent of annual installation revenue and is administered by the Community Programs team.
